Inside the experiment

Physics

The slice is a volumetric soft body: about 4 300 tetrahedra on 1 060 nodes. Each tetrahedron is solved with XPBD using a stable neo-Hookean model — a deviatoric (shape) constraint and a hydrostatic (volume) constraint, solved together — so volume is preserved and flipped elements push themselves back out.

The rind and pale layer are about twice as stiff as the flesh. A fixed 60 Hz step is split into small substeps; internal damping smooths relative node velocity, so motion fades without snapping.

Rendering

Native WebGPU + WGSL. A smooth surface is skinned to the tetrahedra every frame; seeds are rigid bodies attached to four embedded anchors each. The jelly shader marches a short path through the material (flesh, pale layer, skin) between the front and back faces, absorbing light by thickness (Beer–Lambert), refracting the floor with slight dispersion, and adding Fresnel reflections of a procedural studio. Contact shadows come from a height map of the slice.
